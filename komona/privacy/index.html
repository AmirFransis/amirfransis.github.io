1. Who we are

Komona ("Komona", "we", "us") is a mobile app for organizing private events with friends, such as pickup games, dinners and board-game nights. It gives each event a live roster, a first-come-first-served waitlist and shared "asks". It is not a public event marketplace: events are visible only to people invited by link or code.

The data controller is Amir Fransis, an individual developer based in Israel. Contact: fransis.developer@gmail.com.

2. The short version

  • We collect what we need to run your events: your name, photo, email or sign-in ID, the events you create or join, and your spot on rosters and waitlists.
  • Other members of an event can see your display name, photo and RSVP status. People outside the event cannot.
  • We do not use your device's GPS. Event addresses are typed in by organizers.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.
  • You can delete your account at any time in Profile → Delete account or at amirfransis.github.io/komona/delete-account.

3. What we collect

3.1 Account and profile

  • When you sign in with Apple, Google or TikTok: a unique account ID, your name, your email address (Apple may give us a private relay email) and, if available, your profile photo.
  • When you sign in with email: your email address. We send a one-time code to it; we do not store passwords.
  • Profile details you set or edit: display name, photo and language (English or Hebrew).

3.2 Events and activity

  • Events you create: title, description, category, address or venue name, date, time, duration, repeat schedule, roster size and RSVP rules.
  • Your participation: joining or leaving, waitlist position, role (owner, co-host or member), asks you post or claim, and when these actions happened.
  • Invite links and codes you create, use, regenerate or revoke.
  • Simple counts we show you, such as events hosted and events attended.

3.3 Feedback

  • App feedback you send from Profile → Send feedback (idea, bug or praise, and your message).

3.4 Device and technical data

  • A push notification token, so we can send event updates.
  • Device model, operating system, app version, language and time zone.
  • Crash reports and diagnostics (Sentry), and in-app usage events (PostHog), such as screens viewed and actions like creating or joining an event.
  • IP address, as part of normal server and network logs.

4. What other members can see

Komona is social by design. Inside an event you belong to, other members can see your display name, profile photo, whether you are going or on the waitlist (and your position), your role, and asks you have claimed. Organizers can also see the member list and remove members. We never show your email address or sign-in provider to other members.

5. What we do not collect

  • Your precise or background location (GPS).
  • Your contacts or address book. Invites are shared by link or code that you choose to send.
  • Advertising identifiers. We do not run ads or ad tracking.
  • Payment card details. Komona is currently free.
  • The content of your chats in other apps such as WhatsApp or Telegram. We only see that a share sheet was opened, not what you sent.

8. Where your data is stored

Our database is hosted in the United States (AWS, Ohio) and crash reports are stored in the European Union (Germany). Other providers listed above may process data in other countries. Where data leaves Israel or the EEA, we rely on adequacy decisions, Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent safeguards.

10. How we protect your data

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest.
  • Session tokens stored in the device's secure storage.
  • Server-side checks so only event members can read event data, and only organizers can change it.
  • Invite links and codes that organizers can revoke or regenerate at any time.
  • Limited staff access, on a need-to-know basis.

If a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

11. Notifications

We send push notifications about events you belong to: for example, when RSVPs open, when a spot opens for you, when an organizer posts an ask, or when a game is cancelled. You can turn them off in Profile → Notifications or in your phone's settings. We do not send marketing notifications without your consent.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct inaccurate data (most of it you can edit in the app);
  • delete your data;
  • object to or restrict some processing;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent where we rely on it.

Email fransis.developer@gmail.com to make a request. We may need to verify your identity and will reply within the time the law requires.

13. Deleting your account

Self-service
Go to Profile → Delete account in the app, or visit amirfransis.github.io/komona/delete-account without needing to reinstall the app.

Deletion removes your profile, sign-in link, push tokens, the events you own (they are cancelled), and your RSVPs, waitlist spots and asks in other events.

Deletion does not immediately remove encrypted backups (overwritten on their normal cycle), short-lived crash logs at our providers, or records we must keep by law. Feedback you sent is kept without your name.

14. Children

Komona is not intended for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app before they take effect.

16. Contact and complaints

Privacy questions: fransis.developer@gmail.com
