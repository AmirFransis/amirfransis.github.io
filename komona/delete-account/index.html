Delete your account

You can delete your Komona account and its data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

Self-service
Open Komona → Profile → Delete account → confirm. Your account is deleted right away.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email fransis.developer@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Komona account". We will confirm it is you and delete the account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: name, photo and language.
  • Your sign-in link with Apple, Google, TikTok or email.
  • Your push notification tokens.
  • Your spots on rosters and waitlists, and your claims on asks. If you had a spot, the next person on the waitlist moves in.
  • Events you own are cancelled.

What may be kept for a while

  • Encrypted backups, until they are overwritten on their normal cycle.
  • Short-lived crash and diagnostic logs at our providers.
  • Feedback you sent, without your name.
  • Anything we must keep by law.

See the Privacy Policy for details.
